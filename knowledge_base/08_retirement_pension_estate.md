# Retirement, Pension and Estate Planning

## Retirement calculation
A simplified real-value model:
`Required corpus ≈ annual retirement spending gap ÷ assumed real withdrawal rate`.
This is a rough scenario tool, not a guarantee. Results depend on longevity, inflation, sequence-of-returns risk, taxes, healthcare and asset allocation.

## Future retirement spending
`Future expense = current expense × (1+inflation)^years`.

## Pension products
NPS, EPF, PPF, annuities and other schemes have different eligibility, contribution, withdrawal, tax and risk rules. Verify current rules from official scheme administrators and government sources.

## Estate basics
Consider nominations, a will, asset inventory, account access, insurance beneficiaries and family communication. Nomination and legal succession are not always identical; recommend qualified legal advice for complex situations.

## Dynamic verification
Check current PFRDA, EPFO, Department of Economic Affairs, income-tax and scheme-specific rules before giving exact limits, tax treatment or withdrawal advice.
