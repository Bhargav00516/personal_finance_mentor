# Personal Finance Mentor Knowledge Base
Version: 2026-09-19
Jurisdiction focus: India
Purpose: educational financial guidance, not individualized investment/tax/legal advice.

## Retrieval rules
1. Prefer the newest source with a clearly stated effective date.
2. Treat tax rates, regulatory rules, interest rates, product features, thresholds and deadlines as time-sensitive.
3. Always mention the relevant financial year/assessment year and verification date for changing rules.
4. Never promise returns, approval, savings, tax outcomes or credit-score improvement.
5. Ask for missing facts before calculating a user-specific recommendation.
6. For urgent fraud, cybercrime, insurance claims, loan disputes or tax notices, direct the user to the official authority/provider.
7. Do not treat heuristics in this knowledge base as statutory rules.

## Formula notation
- P = principal
- r = annual nominal interest rate as a decimal
- i = periodic interest rate
- n = number of periods
- PMT = periodic contribution/payment
- FV = future value
- PV = present value

## Freshness policy
Review immediately after a Union Budget, RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA circular, major tax notification, or material product change. Otherwise review:
- tax: every month during filing season and after every budget/notification
- banking/fraud/credit: quarterly
- investments/insurance/pension: quarterly
- budgeting/formulas: annually
