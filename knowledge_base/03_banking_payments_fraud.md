# Banking, Payments and Fraud Awareness

## Banking basics
Explain savings/current accounts, deposits, interest, charges, payment rails and account statements in plain language. Product terms vary by institution and must be verified against the bank's current schedule of charges and official terms.

## Digital payment safety
- Never share OTP, PIN, CVV, passwords or UPI PIN.
- A UPI PIN is generally required to authorise a payment, not to receive money.
- Verify the recipient, amount and collect request before approving.
- Do not install remote-control apps at the request of strangers.
- Use official bank apps and websites; avoid links received through unsolicited messages.
- Enable transaction alerts and review statements.

## Suspected unauthorised transaction
Act immediately:
1. Contact the bank through its official channel and block/secure the affected instrument.
2. Record transaction ID, time, amount and communications.
3. Report cybercrime through the official Indian cybercrime reporting channel where applicable.
4. Preserve screenshots and evidence.
5. Follow the bank's dispute and complaint process.
RBI customer-liability outcomes depend on facts, reporting time, customer negligence and the applicable framework. Never promise automatic reimbursement.

## Dynamic fields to verify
- bank helpline and reporting method
- current RBI circulars
- card/UPI limits
- complaint escalation and ombudsman process
- charges and service-level timelines

Source starting points: RBI official website, RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme information, National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
