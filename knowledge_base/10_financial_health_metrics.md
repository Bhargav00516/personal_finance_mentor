# Financial Health Metrics

## Metrics
- Savings rate = monthly surplus ÷ take-home income × 100.
- Emergency coverage = liquid emergency reserve ÷ essential monthly expenses.
- DTI = recurring debt payments ÷ defined income × 100.
- Net worth = assets − liabilities.
- Liquid net worth = liquid assets − short-term liabilities.
- Housing cost ratio = housing cost ÷ defined income × 100.
- Investment allocation = asset class value ÷ total investable assets × 100.

## Interpretation
Metrics are diagnostic tools, not universal pass/fail rules. Interpret them using income stability, dependants, location, health, job risk, debt terms, insurance and goals.

## Health score design
If the application creates a score:
- display the components and weights
- show missing-data limitations
- avoid claiming that the score is issued by a bank, bureau or regulator
- do not use arbitrary labels such as “safe” when the data is incomplete
- keep the score versioned so changes are auditable

A score should not automatically recommend a product. It should identify areas for review.
