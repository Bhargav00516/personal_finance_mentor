# Indian Tax Awareness

## Critical warning
Tax rules change. Always identify:
- financial year/tax year
- assessment year where relevant
- residential status
- taxpayer category
- income heads
- regime selected
- deductions/exemptions applicable
- current notifications and official calculators

This file is an educational framework, not a substitute for current tax computation by a qualified professional.

## Basic computation flow
1. Identify income under applicable heads.
2. Apply permitted exclusions and adjustments.
3. Compute gross total income.
4. Apply eligible deductions under the applicable regime.
5. Compute taxable income.
6. Apply slab/rate provisions, surcharge, cess and relief where applicable.
7. Account for TDS, advance tax, self-assessment tax and credits.
8. Verify return type and filing deadline.

## Common concepts
- Gross income is not automatically taxable income.
- Tax slab rates are marginal: each portion is taxed according to the applicable slab.
- Tax liability and TDS are different concepts.
- Capital gains treatment depends on asset type, holding period, transaction date, exemptions and law applicable at that time.
- Tax treatment of dividends, interest, rent, business income, securities and insurance proceeds can differ.

## Formula concepts
- Tax before credits = sum of tax calculated under applicable slabs/rates.
- Net tax payable = gross tax + applicable additions − eligible reliefs/credits − TDS/advance tax.
- Effective tax rate = total tax ÷ relevant total income × 100.
Do not hard-code slabs in application logic without a dated versioned configuration.

## Official verification
Use the Income Tax Department's current portal, official tax calculator, Finance Act/Income-tax Act provisions, circulars and notifications. For FY/AY-specific answers, cite the exact official page and effective date.
