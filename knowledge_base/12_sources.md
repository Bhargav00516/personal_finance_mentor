# Source Register and Verification Checklist

Use official sources first. Store exact URLs and dates in your ingestion metadata.

## Primary authorities
- Reserve Bank of India: https://www.rbi.org.in/
- SEBI: https://www.sebi.gov.in/
- IRDAI: https://irdai.gov.in/
- PFRDA: https://pfrda.org.in/
- Income Tax Department: https://www.incometax.gov.in/ and https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/
- National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in/
- RBI Sachet/consumer awareness resources where applicable
- Official lender, insurer, fund house and scheme documents for product-specific facts

## Verification checklist
For every time-sensitive claim record:
- claim
- source URL
- authority
- publication date
- effective date
- last checked date
- applicable geography
- applicable financial/tax year
- whether superseded
- reviewer notes

## Source hierarchy
1. Law, regulation, official notification or circular
2. Official authority FAQ/calculator/consumer guidance
3. Official product document
4. Reputable secondary explanation, clearly labelled
5. General educational content

Do not use search snippets alone as evidence. Recheck links and archive document versions when possible.
