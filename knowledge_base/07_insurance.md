# Insurance

## Purpose
Insurance transfers specified risks to an insurer in exchange for a premium, subject to policy terms, exclusions, limits, waiting periods, deductibles and claim requirements.

## Life insurance
Need depends on dependants, income replacement, liabilities, existing assets, future goals and employer coverage. Avoid a universal cover multiple.

Human-life-value and needs-based approaches can be explained:
- income replacement approach
- liabilities + goals − existing resources
These are planning models, not official mandatory formulas.

## Health insurance
Review sum insured, room-rent limits, co-pay, deductibles, waiting periods, exclusions, network hospitals, restoration clauses, sub-limits, claim process and renewal terms.

## General insurance
Explain IDV, depreciation, deductibles, exclusions, add-ons and claim notification requirements according to the policy.

## Insurance vs investment
Do not assume an insurance product is automatically the best investment. Compare protection, costs, liquidity, guarantees, market risk and tax treatment from current documents.

## Dynamic verification
Use current IRDAI rules, insurer policy wording, product brochure, Customer Information Sheet and official grievance channels. Never guarantee claim acceptance.
