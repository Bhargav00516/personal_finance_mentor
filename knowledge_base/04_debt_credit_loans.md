# Debt, Credit Cards, Loans and Credit Reports

## Key definitions
- Principal: amount borrowed.
- Interest: cost of borrowing.
- APR/annualised cost: may include interest and applicable charges depending on the product and disclosure framework.
- EMI: periodic payment containing interest and principal.
- Secured loan: backed by collateral.
- Unsecured loan: generally not backed by specific collateral.
- Fixed rate: rate remains fixed under the contract for the stated period.
- Floating rate: rate may change under contractual benchmark and reset rules.

## EMI formula
For principal P, monthly rate i and n monthly payments:
`EMI = P × i × (1+i)^n / ((1+i)^n − 1)`
If i = 0: `EMI = P/n`.

Total payments = EMI × n.
Approximate total interest = total payments − principal.
Actual amounts can differ because of fees, insurance, rounding, prepayments and rate resets.

## Debt-to-income ratio
`DTI = recurring monthly debt payments ÷ gross or defined monthly income × 100`.
State which income definition is used. DTI is an analytical measure, not a universal approval threshold. Lenders use their own underwriting rules.

## Credit cards
- Paying the full statement balance by the due date generally avoids interest on eligible purchases, subject to card terms.
- Minimum payment is not the same as a debt-free strategy; paying only minimums can extend repayment and increase interest.
- Cash advances can have different fees and interest treatment.
- Check annual fees, late fees, finance charges, reward exclusions and dispute rules.

## Credit score
Credit scores are generated by credit information companies using their models and available data. Factors can include repayment history, utilisation, credit age, account mix and recent applications. Do not promise that one utilisation percentage or one score guarantees approval.
